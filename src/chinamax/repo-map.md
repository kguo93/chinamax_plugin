# repo-map — src/chinamax/

The Runtime — the agent loop that owns one Job's conversation with a provider — plus the Job supervisor that makes a dispatch durable and detached.

- `__init__.py` — package docstring, `ChinamaxError` (the operator-facing failure type, reported at the CLI seam) and `ToolError` (the model-facing tool refusal, rendered as an error observation).
- `__main__.py` — the CLI seam for every verb. `main(argv)` parses `exec`, `task`, `task-worker`, `status` and `logs` and maps failures onto the shared exit codes (0 terminal / 2 active / 1 usage-or-resolution). `execute_spec(spec, config, reporter)` is the shared entry — it sanitizes the ambient `ANTHROPIC_*` variables, resolves the Profile and key, builds the `LoopConfig`, runs the loop and writes the result atomically; `run_exec(spec_path, config)` wraps it for `exec` and is the failure seam (a `RunFailure` becomes one structured reporter line plus exit 1). The supervisor verbs: `run_task()` (fail-fast prompt/workspace/Profile resolution, then the durable record and `_spawn_worker()`'s detached `Popen`), `run_task_worker()` (the compare-and-swap claim, the in-process Runtime call through `execute_spec`, the heartbeat, and the terminal write), `run_status()` (the listing, one Job, and the bounded `--wait` poll) and `run_logs()` (the progress log, falling back to the spawn log when it is empty). Also `PREVIEW_LINES` (4) and `RECENT_LIMIT` (20).
- `state.py` — the durable per-workspace Job store (ADR 0003/0004). Layout and keying: `state_root()` (`CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA` then `XDG_STATE_HOME`, empty or relative counting as unset), `resolve_workspace_root()` (git-toplevel resolution), `workspace_key()`, `open_store()`. `JobStore` owns the paths (`state.json` index, `state.lock` sidecar, and the six per-Job artifacts `<id>.json`/`.log`/`.spawn.log`/`.thread.jsonl`/`.result.json`/`.steer/`), the `O_EXCL` `reserve_id()`, the single locked compare-and-swap `update()`, the filename-derived index rebuild (`job_ids()`, `load_records()`) and the shared `resolve_job()` selector. Plus the record schema (`SCHEMA_VERSION`, `RECORD_DEFAULTS`, `new_record()`, `normalize_record()`), the timing constants (`RECORD_THROTTLE_S` 2 s, `HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_S` 30 s, `POLL_INTERVAL_S` 2 s, `WAIT_TIMEOUT_MS` 240 000), the 0700/0600 helpers (`make_dir`, `precreate`, `secure_file`), the log readers (`log_signature`, `tail_lines`), `escape_control()`/`summarize()`/`render_failure()`, `parse_pid_start_time()`/`read_pid_start_time()`, and the two progress mechanisms `ProgressReporter` and `Heartbeat`.
- `spec.py` — `JobSpec` and the job-spec parser/validator: the public dispatch contract (`workspace`, `profile`, `prompt`, `transcript_path`, `result_path`, optional `write`/`job_id`/`bash_timeout_s` and the supervision overrides `inactivity_timeout_s`/`ladder_attempts`/`backoff_base_s`/`backoff_cap_s`), plus `DEFAULT_BASH_TIMEOUT_S` (600 s).
- `confinement.py` — the tool-layer policy (ADR 0005): `ToolContext` (workspace realpath, posture, bash timeout), `resolve_in_workspace()`/`contained()` (component-wise realpath containment), `lex_command()` → `Stage` (the one shlex-based command lexer), and the two predicates over it — `denied_reason()` (the operator's hard bans) and `write_shaped_reason()` (read-only Jobs).
- `profiles.py` — `Profile`, the shipped-plus-overlay resolution (`load_profiles`, `resolve_profile`, `format_available`) and API-key lookup from `~/.claude/model-keys.env` (`load_keys`, `resolve_key`).
- `provider.py` — `sanitize_environment()` and `build_client()`: the `anthropic` SDK client for one Profile (bearer auth, `max_retries=0`, and a structured `httpx.Timeout` whose read budget backstops the inactivity watchdog).
- `liveness.py` — the supervision layer (ADR 0002): `LoopConfig` + `build_config()` (the inactivity/ladder/backoff knobs and the injected clock, sleeper and jitter seams), `InactivityWatchdog`, `run_attempt()` (one streaming call, all-or-nothing at `message_stop`), `classify()` with the transient/permanent table, `parse_retry_after()`, `backoff_sleep()`, `stream_with_ladder()` (the ladder itself), `RunFailure` (the terminal failure payload) and `emit_event()` (the structured progress reporter).
- `loop.py` — `run_loop()`: the streaming Messages tool-use loop, the system prompt, the tool-less-turn nudge, and the report_result termination rules. Builds the Job's posture-filtered registry and its `ToolContext` once, then routes every tool_use through them; each turn goes through `liveness.stream_with_ladder`. Also the closed progress vocabulary `PHASES` (`PHASE_STARTING`, `PHASE_CALLING_MODEL`, `PHASE_RUNNING_TOOL`, `PHASE_REPORTING`) and the optional `reporter` seam the supervisor mirrors into a Job's log and record.
- `transcript.py` — `Transcript` (write-ahead JSONL writer, with `append_message()` and the non-replayable `append_retry()`) and `read_messages()` (the replay reader), over the versioned `{v, ts, kind}` record schema.
- `tools/` — the tool registry advertised to the provider. See `tools/repo-map.md`.
- `data/profiles.json` — the five shipped pro Profile rows (deepseek, mimo, glm, minimax, kimi), each `{name, base_url, model, api_key_env}`; `max_tokens` is an optional per-row override of the code default.
